About Mosaic

At Mosaic, we believe that every artist is a unique piece of a larger creative puzzle. Founded with a passion for discovering and nurturing exceptional talent, we specialize in representing writers, directors, and visionary creators who shape the future of entertainment and storytelling.

Founded by Jayesh Bagrecha, a legal and business professional turned literary representative, Mosaic combines creative insight with strategic positioning to guide screenwriters through packaging, development, and studio outreach.

We work closely with production studios and creative partners, understanding their need for distinctive yet market-ready content, and offering access to writers whose voices elevate stories with both artistic depth and audience appeal.
