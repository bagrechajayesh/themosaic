About Mosaic

At Mosaic, we believe that every artist is a unique piece of a larger creative puzzle. Founded with a passion for discovering and nurturing exceptional talent, we specialize in representing writers, directors, and visionary creators who shape the future of entertainment and storytelling.

Our boutique approach ensures personalized attention for each client, combining industry expertise with genuine care for artistic vision. We don't just manage careers – we build lasting partnerships that honor the creative process while achieving commercial success.

With deep connections across film, television, publishing, and digital media, Mosaic opens doors that matter and creates opportunities that align with our artists' unique voices and ambitions.

Arvind Sivakumaran

Film maker - Scholar - Writer

Arvind graduated in Film Production from Victoria Motion Picture School, B.C., Canada in 2002. He also holds a degree in English Literature from St. Xaviers College, Mumbai. 1) His screenplay “Middle of Nowhere” was shortlisted for the Quarter-finals of the Nicholl (Academy of Motion Pictures Science and Arts A.M.P.A.S) Fellowship, Hollywood California. It was one of only 368 scripts that were selected from 7197 submitted entries. 2) His short film " J'aurais Toujous Paris (I'll Always Have Paris) " was in THE VIEWING ROOM at IFFI (International Film Festival Of India) 2016. It also won 'BEST FILM NOIR SHORT" at TOP SHORTS 2017, an international online festival. It was also nominated for Best Editing at the International Horror Movie Awards 2016. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fREmhLq_y-w&t=234s 3) Has been a Guest lecturer in Film with a focus on American film and editing/sound at St. Xaviers College Mumbai during 2004 - 05 and at M.E.T. (Mumbai Educational Trust) during 2011-16. 4) Film Curator and facilitator for the Sunaparanta Center for the Arts, Altinho, Goa during 2016-19 5) Senior Development Executive and Writer, Culture Machine Studios during 2020-21 6) His original noir play “My Funny Valentine” won the Best Play award at Thespo 2000, an annual theatre Festival in Mumbai.
